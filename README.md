# 🍽️ Food Companion

> **Discover food. Cook it. Experience it. Remember it.**

Food Companion is an AI-powered personal food discovery and memory platform that helps you decide what to eat based on your mood, explore different cuisines, learn how to prepare dishes, analyze your food photos, record your real eating experience, and build a personal food journal.

## ✨ What It Does

Food Companion connects the entire food journey in one place:

**DISCOVER → EXPLORE → COOK → PHOTOGRAPH → ANALYZE → RATE → REMEMBER → LEARN**

### 🧠 Mood-Based Food Discovery

Tell Food Companion how you feel or what you're craving, and explore food recommendations based on your current mood and preferences.

Examples:

- 😌 Comforting
- 🔥 Spicy
- 🥗 Healthy
- 🍰 Indulgent
- ⚡ Energetic
- 🌿 Refreshing
- 🌎 Adventurous

You can also discover food based on cuisine, ingredients, dietary preferences, meal type, cooking time, difficulty, and other preferences.

### 🌍 Explore Cuisines

Browse food from different regions and cuisines around the world, with dedicated food imagery and detailed information for individual dishes.

### 👨‍🍳 Detailed Cooking Guides

Select a dish and get beginner-friendly preparation instructions designed to help you actually make the food in your own kitchen.

Recipes can include:

- Ingredients
- Equipment
- Preparation
- Step-by-step cooking
- Cooking times
- Serving information
- Substitutions
- Cooking tips
- Common mistakes
- Variations
- Flavor and texture information

### 📸 AI Food Analysis

Upload or photograph your food and let the system analyze what can reasonably be observed from the image.

The analysis can include:

- Food identification
- Confidence
- Visual presentation
- Color
- Composition
- Visible texture
- Plating
- Visual observations
- Possible improvements

The AI is designed to distinguish between **what it can see** and **what the user actually experiences**.

### ⭐ AI vs Human Rating

Food Companion keeps AI analysis separate from your personal experience.

**AI evaluates what it can SEE.**

**You evaluate what you EXPERIENCE.**

Rate your food based on:

- Taste
- Aroma
- Texture
- Presentation
- Overall experience

Add personal notes such as:

> "Very spicy but delicious."

> "Perfect texture."

> "Would definitely order again."

### 📖 Personal Food Journal

Save your food experiences as visual memories.

Each journal entry can contain:

- Food photograph
- Dish
- Cuisine
- Date
- Mood
- AI visual score
- Personal ratings
- Notes

Over time, your journal becomes a personal history of the food you've experienced.

### 📊 Personal Food Insights

As your journal grows, Food Companion can identify patterns from your actual recorded experiences.

For example:

- Your highest-rated cuisines
- Your average food rating
- Your favorite dishes
- Foods you eat most frequently
- Your preferred textures
- Your preferred spice levels
- The cuisines you've explored
- Changes in your food preferences over time

The system should only generate insights from information actually recorded in your journal.

## 🎯 Core Idea

Food Companion is **not just a recipe app**.

It is **not just an AI food scanner**.

It is **not just a food diary**.

It combines food discovery, cooking, visual analysis, personal ratings, and food memories into one connected experience.

The goal is to turn food from a **static image or recipe into an interactive experience**.
